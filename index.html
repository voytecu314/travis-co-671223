02 / The long way round

Locals at heart.
Curious by nature.

Travis&Co started with a borrowed canoe, a paper map and a question: what if the best bits were just off the trail?

Today, we're a small crew of guides, climbers and very enthusiastic soup-makers. We build thoughtful trips into the caves and lake country of the Nordic north—always at a human pace, always with care for the places that let us in.
